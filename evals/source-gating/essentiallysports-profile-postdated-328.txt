UFC 328 walkout songs: entrance music linked to several featured fighters after Saturday's card in Newark.

While the event is now over, several athletes are still associated with the songs they have used across previous UFC appearances.

  * Alexander Volkov: "KATYUSHA" by Alex Terrible
  * Sean Brady: "They Don't Love You No More" by DJ Khaled feat. Jay Z, Meek Mill, Rick Ross & French Montana
  * Joel Alvarez: "Cancion del Mariachi" by Antonio Banderas

These are the tracks the fighters are known for and have used before, rather than a confirmed list of what each athlete walked out to on the night.
